How to review (5 minutes)

  • Open the prototype (best on a phone). Walk the onboarding — disclaimer, a real one-minute contemplation, plan selection (purchases are simulated, nothing is charged), account, and intake.
  • From Home, press Begin — the daily loop: series introduction, timer & music setup, the contemplation itself, and the thought diary (write or record a voice note).
  • To see a full series arc without waiting seven days: Menu → Settings → Fast-forward one contemplation (a testing tool, hidden in release builds), then finish the last day to reach the wrap-up, celebration stats, and completion survey.
  • To start over: Settings → Delete account. All data lives privately on your device only.

What's working today

  • All 23 V1 screens, fully functional and navigable
  • Core practice loop: chosen timer, video + music atmosphere, uninterruptible contemplation, add-time, journal with 150-word cap and 60-second voice memos (live soundwave, replay/re-record)
  • Reflections saved privately and revealed at series completion, with real computed stats and streaks
  • Series gating & mock monetization matching the business model ($7.99 pack / $21.99 monthly / $89.99 annual with 3-day trial)
  • Design system v1: brand typography, olive/stone palette, bottom navigation — applied live across the app
  • Safety: content advisory, 12+ gate, persistent crisis button with one-tap call/text to 988 and Crisis Text Line
